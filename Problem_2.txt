## How to Run

# First-time setup: see solutions/problem_2/setup.txt
  cd ~/bda-exercises-4-group-1
  sbatch solutions/problem_2/run_taxi.slurm
Results are written to slurm-bda-taxi-<jobid>.out and to
solutions/problem_2/taxi_<timestamp>.log (flushed line-by-line).

Required data files (relative to the repo root):
  data/problem_2/trip_data-part*.csv
  data/problem_2/nyc-borough-boundaries-polygon.geojson.json

## (d) Outlier detection -- trips above 95th-percentile normalised duration
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Normalisation: duration (seconds) / Haversine distance (km).
95th-percentile threshold and outlier count per starting borough:

Borough          Threshold (s/km)   Outlier trips
Bronx                   7,235.60             374
Brooklyn                  448.28          15,987
Manhattan                 578.84         665,548
Queens                    339.22          33,426
Staten Island          14,839.72              17

Outlier counts equal exactly 5 % of each borough's trip population by construction.
The large absolute count for Manhattan (665 k) reflects its dominance of the
dataset (~85 % of all trips). The extremely high thresholds for Bronx and Staten
Island indicate these boroughs have many short-distance, very slow trips (e.g.,
stop-and-go traffic on short hops) that push the 95th percentile up to values
that would be outliers in denser boroughs.

Over the full dataset the pre-dawn hours (03-05) are the slowest in terms of
normalised duration, not the evening rush. This reflects the influence of
overnight airport trips (long distance to JFK/LGA but dense stop-and-go on
local roads raises the ratio). The morning rush (07-09) is clearly visible as
the next cluster of elevated values. The evening rush (16-18) is present but
dampened relative to the morning rush, likely because Manhattan drivers disperse
outward (faster highway segments) rather than concentrating inward. Hour 21 is
the fastest hour (lowest normalised duration), consistent with light late-night
traffic on well-travelled routes.
