Input data
- Source: Yahoo Finance chart API daily historical prices, used as an equivalent public historical CSV source.
- Download period requested: 2021-06-04 to 2026-06-04.
- Stock CSVs: data/problem_3/stocks/AAPL.csv, data/problem_3/stocks/MSFT.csv, data/problem_3/stocks/NVDA.csv
- Factor CSVs: data/problem_3/factors/SP500.csv, data/problem_3/factors/NASDAQ100.csv, data/problem_3/factors/DOWJONES.csv
- The script downloads JSON from the chart API and stores local CSV files with Date, Open, High, Low, Close, Adj Close, and Volume columns.

Data quality and temporal alignment
- Rows per file before alignment: 1,255 for each of AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, SP500, NASDAQ100, and DOWJONES.
- Common aligned date range: 2021-06-04 to 2026-06-03.
- Aligned rows: 1,255.
- Return rows after 10-trading-day lag: 1,245.
- Two-week returns are computed as close_t / close_{t-10} - 1.

Modeling methodology
- Each CSV is first parsed through a Spark RDD stage, then converted to a DataFrame for Spark ML.
- Factor features are built from each factor return r as r, r^2, and sqrt(abs(r)).
- The three factors therefore produce 9 features per row.
- One Spark ML LinearRegression model is trained for each stock return.
- The correlated-factor simulation samples from MultivariateNormal(mu, Sigma), where mu and Sigma are the historical factor-return mean vector and covariance matrix.
- The independent-factor simulation samples each factor separately from Normal(mu_j, sigma_j), ignoring off-diagonal covariance terms.
- VaR_95 is the lower 5% quantile of simulated two-week returns.
- CVaR_95 is the average simulated two-week return at or below VaR_95.

Monte Carlo settings
- Trials: 1,000,000.
- Partitions: 10.
- Random seed: 42.
- Quantile error: 0.0.

Best/worst stock interpretation
- Best investment by downside-risk criterion: AAPL, because it has the least negative CVaR and VaR among the three stocks.
- Worst investment by downside-risk criterion: NVDA, because it has the most negative CVaR and VaR, even though its mean simulated return is the highest.
- This interpretation prioritizes downside risk over expected return because the assignment asks for VaR/CVaR-based risk analysis.

Independent-factor portfolio results
- Mean two-week portfolio return: 0.012955 (1.2955%).
- VaR_95: -0.076045 (-7.6045%).
- CVaR_95: -0.099707 (-9.9707%).

Comparison of correlated vs independent factors
- The independent-factor assumption produced a slightly worse portfolio VaR and CVaR.
- Correlated factors: VaR_95 = -7.2171%, CVaR_95 = -9.5390%.
- Independent factors: VaR_95 = -7.6045%, CVaR_95 = -9.9707%.
- In this run, removing factor correlations increased estimated downside risk by about 0.39 percentage points in VaR and 0.43 percentage points in CVaR.
